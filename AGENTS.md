# Weekly task attribution

The user-confirmed convention is that a dated weekly folder or Google Sheets tab starts with that date's meeting and contains the work assigned at that meeting for the period afterward.

- `2026-10-11`: tasks assigned at the October 11 meeting, to work on after October 11.
- `2026-10-15`: tasks assigned at the October 15 meeting, to work on after October 15.
- `2026-10-22`: tasks assigned at the October 22 meeting, to work on after October 22.

Attribute tasks by the meeting that assigned them, not by their deadline or the next meeting date. Do not move October 15 follow-up tasks to October 22. The Google Drive task tracker uses the same convention.

Keep `meeting.md`, `planning.md`, and `research.md` together under the assigning meeting's date. Preserve original task ownership when work remains unfinished; later weeks may link to it in Carryover with the original meeting date and confirmed status. Do not silently duplicate an earlier assignment as a new current-week task or infer completion.

Read `docs/weeks/README.md` before updating weekly records or the Google Drive task tracker.
