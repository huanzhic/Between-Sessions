## Status guide

- **Planned** — identified, not yet scheduled for reading.
- **To read** — selected for an upcoming research task.
- **Read** — reviewed; findings may or may not be relevant.
- **Used** — cited in a synthesis, decision, or project deliverable.

“Used” records citation in a team document; it does not imply a complete review of the source. See the review and notes columns for the extent of reading.

Keep the source's citation here. Put week-specific findings and limitations in the linked dated `research.md` record.
