# Weekly Records

Keep one folder per meeting cycle under `docs/weeks/`. Name it with the meeting date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format, normally Thursday, for example `2026-10-15/`. The kickoff meeting retains its actual date, `2026-10-11/`.

## Task ownership by meeting date

The folder date marks the beginning of the work period after that meeting, not the deadline or the end of the preceding week. This user-confirmed rule also applies to dated tabs in the Google Drive task tracker.

| Folder / tab | Tasks belonging here |
| --- | --- |
| `2026-10-11` | Assigned on October 11, to work on after October 11 |
| `2026-10-15` | Assigned on October 15, to work on after October 15 |
| `2026-10-22` | Assigned on October 22, to work on after October 22 |

Keep each meeting's follow-up tasks in that meeting's folder/tab even if they are due at the next meeting or later. October 15 assignments belong in `2026-10-15`, not `2026-10-22`. A deadline does not determine the task's weekly location. Leave unspecified deadlines unspecified.

Keep unfinished tasks in their original record. If needed, link to them from a later record's Carryover section, identifying the assigning meeting and confirmed status. Do not present them as newly assigned tasks or assume that they are complete.

Each week folder contains three records:

- `planning.md` — goals, tasks assigned at the dated meeting for the period afterward, owners, expected outputs, and preparation for the next meeting.
- `research.md` — research questions, search approach, sources reviewed, findings, limitations, and open questions.
- `meeting.md` — concise meeting summary, decisions, unresolved items, and follow-up actions.

Copy the three starter files from [`TEMPLATE/`](TEMPLATE/) into the new dated folder. Link the records to one another so a reader can follow the week's plan, research, and meeting outcomes.

Register each source once in the [source index](../research/sources/source-index.md). Use its stable ID (such as `S001`) in weekly research records rather than repeating the full citation. Add the dated weekly folder to the source index's “Used in weekly records” column when relevant.

Only include meeting summaries and research material approved for this repository. Do not add private correspondence, meeting recordings, real client data, or identifiable sensitive research data.
